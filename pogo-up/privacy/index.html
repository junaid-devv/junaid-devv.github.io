1. Who is responsible

POGO UP (Android package com.pogoup.game) is a precision platform game developed and published by Muhammad Junaid. This policy covers the game and this support website. For privacy questions, email junaidofficial130@gmail.com.

Game features and service availability can differ between versions, regions, and test releases. The initial internal setup build has advertising and purchases disabled. The descriptions below also cover subsequent versions in which those services are enabled.

2. Information the game handles

The game saves completed routes, checkpoints, medals, collectibles, Silver, cosmetic selections, settings, daily reward allowances, and recovery state locally. We do not operate a cloud-save server or upload your campaign save to a developer server.

POGO UP does not ask for your real name, date of birth, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise GPS location. It has no player messaging, public profiles, or user-uploaded content. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and your email address.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

Free play may include interstitial ads between completed routes or at a paused recovery break. Rewarded ads are optional and may provide a level skip or Silver. We request non-personalized ads and apply restrictive under-age-of-consent ad treatment to all players because the game does not collect ages. Such ads can still use information for delivery, basic measurement, frequency limits, security, and fraud prevention; non-personalized does not mean no data processing.

Where applicable, Google's User Messaging Platform presents consent or privacy choices. Open Settings â†’ Ad privacy choices to revisit available choices. Ad availability depends on region, consent status, network conditions, and provider settings. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID through Android settings.

Premium removes in-game ad displays and ad loading after paid access is verified. Purchase verification still runs to maintain that access. Previous advertising records are governed by the provider's retention rules.

4. Premium purchases

Premium is a permanent, non-consumable purchase offered through Google Play, with a US price of $1.99 and regional pricing shown by the store before purchase. Google handles payment details; we do not receive your full card number. RevenueCat verifies the purchase and whether the premium entitlement is active.

Use Restore Purchases to recover eligible Premium access for your store account. Restoration does not recover local campaign saves. Refunds and revoked purchases may change entitlement access. Purchase and refund requests are subject to Google Play's rules.

5. Providers and international processing

We use Google AdMob and Google's consent tools for advertising and privacy choices, Google Play for transactions and distribution, and RevenueCat for purchase verification and access management. These providers may process information on infrastructure outside your country. We do not sell campaign saves or maintain our own advertising audience database.

Read the providers' policies: Google Privacy Policy, Google advertising information, and RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Disclosures may also occur when necessary to comply with applicable obligations or protect users and services against fraud.

6. Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until you clear the game's storage or remove the app. These actions can permanently erase progress and earned items, so use them only when you intend to reset your save. They do not automatically delete store transaction records or RevenueCat records.

We keep support correspondence while resolving the request and as reasonably needed for follow-up, disputes, security, or legal obligations. Purchase and advertising information is retained under the relevant providers' policies and applicable transaction, fraud-prevention, and legal requirements.

To request deletion of data we control, email us with the subject POGO UP data deletion. If your request concerns a purchase, include the Google Play order ID if available. Do not include payment details. We may request minimal information to verify and locate the relevant record. Anonymous records cannot always be linked to you without an identifier. We can remove identifiable RevenueCat customer records under our control where appropriate, but some transaction records may need to be retained. For Google's records or advertising controls, use Google's privacy tools or contact Google.

7. Intended audience

POGO UP is intended for players aged 13 and older. Its precision routes, machinery timing, and progression systems are designed for teens and adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek their personal information. We do not collect birth dates or verify players' ages. If you believe a child has supplied personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address records we control.

The intended audience is separate from a store content rating. Parents and guardians should supervise device, advertising, and purchase settings.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may be able to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing, withdraw consent, or contact your local data-protection authority. Email us to make a request. We will assess it under applicable requirements. Withdrawal does not change processing already carried out. Ads and store purchases may require an internet connection; ordinary gameplay and local saves do not require a game account.

GitHub may handle connection and security logs, including IP addresses, to provide the hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links and email services have their own privacy practices.
